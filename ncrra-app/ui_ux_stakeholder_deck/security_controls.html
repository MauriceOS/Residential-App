Member trust layer

Security is designed into the member experience

Members should understand what data is used, why it is needed and where they can review or change the controls that affect their account.

Experience promiseUse the minimum information needed for a member task, explain the purpose in context, and retain a visible path to account and privacy controls.
01 · CONNECTIONS

Provider details are masked by default

Saved meter and account references are displayed as partial identifiers and are selected from a controlled member list rather than re-entered in every request.

02 · CONSENT

Sharing purpose is stated before routing

The issue flow explains when NCRRA needs to use a saved service reference and restricts its stated purpose to routing that request.

03 · ACCOUNT

Low-friction protection remains visible

Biometric access, password recovery, active-device review and notification settings live in a clear Account area rather than behind support channels.

04 · RECOVERY

Sensitive actions stay understandable

Payment confirmation, password recovery and device changes should use short explanatory copy, a secure provider step where relevant and an auditable outcome.

Implementation rule: the UI does not claim to secure the system by itself; every visible control must map to the approved identity, RBAC, audit and provider-integration controls in the implementation blueprint.
