Home experience

The home screen makes value immediate

Members should immediately see status and the next task.

Identity

Membership status creates immediate confidence

The active member state, member ID and membership card link reassure members that they are in the correct NCRRA account.

Action

Four priority tasks are available at a glance

Report an issue, track tickets, pay a contribution and reuse provider connections are visible before less-frequent features.

Context

Active work and association activity stay visible

Open ticket state, announcements and member benefits show that the app is both a service channel and an active association space.

Home design rule: useful status first; discovery second; promotional content never displaces a member’s current task.
