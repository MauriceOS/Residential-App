# NCRRA Member Services Prototype — Design Direction

## Reference-led direction

The supplied NCRRA Home screen is the ground-truth visual specification. The clickable prototype must preserve its warm off-white canvas, deep navy typography, quiet teal action color, small amber/coral status accents, thin line icons, rounded practical surfaces and content-led hierarchy. Fidelity to this reference takes priority over generic mobile-dashboard patterns.

## Chosen approach: Trusted Member Utility

### Design Movement

Contemporary civic-service design adapted to a members-only association: calm, legible, purposeful and quietly premium rather than decorative.

### Core Principles

1. **Service before spectacle:** every screen makes the next member action unambiguous.
2. **Trust is visible:** show consent, payment purpose, provider status and ticket responsibility in the interface rather than hiding them in legal copy.
3. **Progressive disclosure:** show the important state first and defer administrative detail until a member requests it.
4. **Association context is constant:** NCRRA identity and active membership are present without dominating the working screen.

### Color Philosophy

Warm ivory acts as the calm, low-noise surface. Deep navy carries seriousness and readability. Teal denotes member-initiated action, confirmation and progress. Amber denotes pending work and coral is reserved for a member-facing risk or irreversible action. Color never substitutes for text labels or status icons.

### Layout Paradigm

The phone canvas uses a vertically paced service journal: clear headers, one contextual decision at a time, and long-form operational cards only where a member needs status/history. The desktop prototype presents the phone in a focused device stage with a compact flow rail, not a website dashboard.

### Signature Elements

1. A thin teal progress stroke for the active area or current task.
2. Circular line-icon medallions for service categories and status.
3. Soft white cards on an ivory field, with sparse dividers and restrained elevation.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel affirmative and recoverable. Navigation is direct; task progression is linear when submitting an issue or paying a contribution; safe actions update immediately; sensitive actions make the data purpose visible first.

### Animation

Screen changes use short 180–240ms opacity/translate transitions. Buttons compress slightly on press. The active bottom-navigation mark moves subtly. No looping animation, parallax, neon glow or attention-seeking effects.

### Typography System

Use **Manrope** for compact labels, utility text and body copy, paired with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for high-weight page titles and important monetary/status values. Headings are navy and strong; utility copy is slate; all text maintains accessible contrast.

### Brand Essence

**NCRRA is the member’s trusted place to resolve local services, manage membership and use association value without navigating fragmented group chats.**

Personality: **reassuring, capable, orderly**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are concise and action-oriented. CTAs explain the result, not the mechanism.

Examples: “Report a service issue” and “Track what happens next”.

### Wordmark & Logo

Use the NCRRA text mark in deep navy with a compact line-based community/home emblem. The mark should remain useful without relying on generated typography art.

### Signature Brand Color

**NCRRA Teal — #007B78.**

## Style Decisions

- Preserve the visual language of the supplied NCRRA Home screen across every clickable flow.
- Build the prototype as a member-facing mobile experience shown within a desktop review stage.
- Keep the app useful under low attention and low-bandwidth conditions: no visual clutter, no hidden action labels, no dependence on dense imagery.
