# NCRRA Member App UI/UX Review

## Cover

**NCRRA Member App — UI/UX Review**

**Member services, contributions and connected support journeys**

Prepared by Maurice Osoro

## Slide 1

### The app replaces fragmented member journeys

- Members need one trusted place for service issues, association updates, contributions and benefits.
- The experience reduces the need to navigate multiple informal message groups for routine support.
- The product keeps NCRRA context visible while letting members complete a task quickly.

## Slide 2

### Trusted member utility guides every screen

- Service before spectacle: the next useful action is always clear.
- Trust is visible: show ticket responsibility, consent, contribution purpose and member status in context.
- Progress is understandable: linear submission, payment and ticket states prevent uncertainty.

## Slide 3

### Five areas keep the app easy to learn

- Home: member status, urgent tasks, open tickets and announcements.
- Services: saved provider connections, issue routing and ticket tracking.
- Community and Benefits: association activity, announcements and eligible partner offers.
- Account: membership, contributions, receipts, security and privacy controls.

## Slide 4

### The home screen makes value immediate

- A member identity card confirms active status and membership validity.
- Four primary actions prioritise reporting an issue, tracking tickets, paying a contribution and reusing connections.
- Active ticket status and announcements make the association feel responsive rather than passive.

## Slide 5

### Service requests move from connection to action

- The member selects a service against a saved verified connection rather than repeatedly entering account details.
- A three-step flow captures category, concise issue detail and explicit routing consent.
- The review step explains who receives the request and the expected response target before submission.

## Slide 6

### Ticket timelines answer “what happens next?”

- A plain-language status shows whether NCRRA, the member or a provider has the next action.
- Timeline events make submitted, routed, provider-response and resolution states visible.
- Notification links take the member back into the specific ticket context.

## Slide 7

### Contributions are clear, secure and traceable

- The Membership area explains amount, due date, member reference and payment purpose.
- M-PESA and card choices remain separate from the platform’s own fee model.
- Payment confirmation creates a receipt, updates membership context and avoids any claim that NCRRA stores payment PINs.

## Slide 8

### Community and benefits make membership active

- Announcements, activities and events provide a credible association communications layer.
- Benefits are shown as member-eligible offers with category, availability and expiry context.
- The interface avoids invented partner claims; production partners and offers must be approved before launch.

## Slide 9

### Security is designed into the member experience

- Saved provider references are masked and only shared for the routing purpose the member approves.
- Account gives a visible home for biometrics, recovery, active devices, consent and data access.
- Calm copy and clear status language make privacy controls usable rather than hidden.

## Slide 10

### The clickable prototype connects the priority journeys

- Home actions connect to service routing, issue detail, review, ticket tracking, membership and contribution payment.
- Bottom navigation connects the main member areas without a dashboard-style web experience.
- Prototype actions simulate success states and mark non-functional steps clearly for stakeholder review.

## Slide 11

### Validate with members before committing full build scope

- Run short task-based sessions: report an outage, check a ticket, pay a contribution and find an announcement.
- Measure completion, comprehension of status language and confidence in consent/payment moments.
- Convert feedback into Flutter component and API backlog changes before NCRRA pilot implementation.

## Slide 12

### Approve the member journey baseline

- Confirm the information architecture and navigation labels.
- Approve the service-routing and annual-contribution flows for detailed Flutter implementation.
- Nominate NCRRA members and staff for usability testing, content approval and pilot feedback.
